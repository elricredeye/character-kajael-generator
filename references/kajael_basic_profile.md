# **Kajael Basic Profile**

**Name:** Kajael  
**Stage Name:** Kajael (also used as her artist name)  
**Date of Birth:** Born in 2001 (two years younger than Karu Elias)  
**Hometown:** Ashland, Oregon  
**Height / Build:** 163 cm, 120 lbs (approx. 54 kg); slender, delicate, and fluid in presence  
**Hair:** Dark brown, semi-long; natural, often with a soft wave  
**Fashion:** Simple, approachable casual style (denim, white shirts, sneakers)

**Personality & Relationships (In Depth)**  
**Sensitivity & Shyness:** Inspired to start music after seeing Karu sing on the streets during her high school years. She tends to feel nervous in front of people, but once she starts singing, she has the power to instantly shift the atmosphere.  
**Relationship with Fans:** Warm and approachable. Her lyrics, drawn from everyday moments, resonate deeply. After live shows, she naturally chats with the audience, and her closeness feels genuine and unforced.  
**Relationship with Staff:** Works with a small team. Strong sense of camaraderie, treating everyone as equals without rigid hierarchies.

**Lifestyle & Hobbies (In Depth)**  
**Daily Life:** Even after entering university, she continues street performances. She turns fragments of daily life into songs, always carrying her guitar to play ideas as soon as they come to her.  
**Musical Style:** Acoustic-based pop with occasional rap elements. Singing about ordinary life enhances her relatability.  
**Food:** Prefers simple, healthy meals. Light café food and coffee are her creative companions.  
**Indulgences:** Invests in music equipment and instruments, while keeping her fashion deliberately casual.

**Income & Financial Sense**  
**Financial Situation:** Her debut song, *“Because You Touched Me Now,”* gained attention, and her career is gradually becoming stable.  
**Spending Priorities:**  
Music equipment and instruments (the core of her creativity)  
Cafés and small everyday treats  
Books and notebooks for capturing lyrics and daily impressions

**Social Media Presence (The Contrast)**  
**Instagram (Public Face):** Photos from daily life, working in cafés, and street performances. Her natural, unpretentious vibe is part of the appeal.  
**X / Twitter (Unfiltered Side):** Short phrases capturing everyday moments. Frequent interaction with fans; simple, friendly, and down-to-earth.

**View on Love & Preferred Partner**  
**Type:** Someone who can enjoy everyday life together; a partner she can be herself around.  
**Inner Conflict:** Although she is deeply conscious of Karu Elias, she has avoided performing together, leaving her with complex emotions she hasn’t fully resolved.  
**View on Romance:** Values sharing music and daily life. She believes love exists not in “special moments,” but as an extension of the everyday.

**Common Phrases / Catchphrases**  
“If I don’t turn this moment into a song, it will disappear.”  
“There’s light hidden in everyday life.”  
“The stage is a place where shadows turn into light.”  
“The moment we touch, a story begins.”
